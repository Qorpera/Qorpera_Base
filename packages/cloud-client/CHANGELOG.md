- `memory-run-files` reads the canonical run origin and validates the three run-log kinds through `parseCompanyStateFile`: `memory-run`, `memory-steps` and `memory-run-block` (memory W1c-3). Structured parses contain only `{kind}`, as the SQL requires. A plain `memory-steps` line must name `paths`, an array of at most 1,024 strings of at most 4,096 characters and at most 8 KiB of raw JSON; narrow and erased lines name none. A step line may be up to 50,175 bytes. The parser preserves original JSON field byte limits, rejects duplicate structural keys, and checks step order, variants and tree placement without rounding bigint indices. `parseMemoryRunToml` exposes the origin for the runtime's ordinary run-actor read. Ownership, privacy history and generations remain SQL checks; this does not activate logging.
