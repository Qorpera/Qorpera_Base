# Changelog

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Buy computer hours in Settings → Plan & usage (and on the account page) once payments open (migration `20260928248910_runtime_computer_hours_payments`, Edge Function `computer-billing`). A plan on sale says "Choose Basis" or "Choose Standard" and opens Stripe's checkout page; a member who pays switches plan ("Switch to Standard"), changes the card and finds invoices on Stripe's billing page ("Payments and invoices"). Plan prices and hours come from the cloud's catalog, which is what Stripe charges (qorpera.com's prices until the cloud has one). The plan card then shows the paid plan, when it renews or ends, that it is paused, or that its payment failed; the hours card shows the plan's hours used of the period's hours, by company. Founders get "Buy hours" on the company's hours card: packs of 20, 100 or 500 hours for the company, valid for 12 months. Stripe sends the member back to the account page, which thanks them and reads the plan again a moment later; the note goes when they leave the page. Card data never passes through the app, and only Stripe's own checkout and billing pages are opened. Before live payments only test accounts an operator enrolled see anything for sale, marked "Test mode: nothing is charged", and a plan's hours stay hidden, since members see only measured hours until then. Members who are not founders now read "Computer hours are paid by the founders. You are never charged." on the company's hours card.
- Settings → Plan & usage shows the open company's computer hours this month, beside its storage: the hours measured so far (one computer hour is an hour of a standard machine of 4 vCPU and 8 GB), "Measured, not charged yet", and what they went to (company machines, agent runs, coding machines, agents' machines). It reads `qorpera_read_company_computer_hours` (migration `20260928248000_runtime_computer_hours_meter`); until the cloud has it the card says "Not measured yet", and a failed lookup says "Computer hours unavailable" without asking again. The plan's own card is now "Computer hours in your plan" and reads "Counted once plans open", and Basis offers "74 computer hours a month" instead of "a worker computer for every agent".
- A coding session's status line says where it is between turns: standing by, running N commands, or asleep; the owner can archive it (the Archive pill beside the status), and a message reopens it. Archived, reopened and "the machine went to sleep" show as notes in the session's thread with how many commands were stopped. An archived session no longer counts as going in the thread's header. Until the cloud has the standby migration, sessions show as before and Archive is not offered.
- Group chats work like agent chats (migration `20260928030000_company_sidebar_group_chats`). Right-click a group chat in the rail for the same menu as an agent: Pin, Move to (a section, Unassigned or a new section), Rename, Copy conversation ID, Hide from sidebar and Delete. Drag it to reorder it, move it to another section or pin it; pinned group chats stand as square cards with their figures beside the pinned agents, and hidden ones fold into "Hidden" (renamed from "Hidden agents"). Group chats and agents share one order in each section; placement is the company's, pins and hidden are yours, as for agents. Rename and Delete are for owners and admins, as every group change is; Delete removes the group chat for everyone after a confirmation. The composer takes images in group chats (up to 5, 8 MiB in all, as in an agent's thread), and ··· → Model sets the model, effort and speed (Fast, on by default) for every agent in the group chat, from the next message (until one is chosen it reads "Each agent's own": each agent runs its own model). A post into a room, an agent's thread included, needs a message: images without text keep Send greyed out ("Add a message to send with the images.") instead of uploading and failing. Until the cloud has the migration, a group chat's menu offers Rename, Copy conversation ID and Delete, the group chat stays after the Unassigned agents, and images stay off.
- Mention people and agents with "@". Typing "@" in a conversation lists who can be named: in a group chat its agents (with their figures) and its other members, in an agent's thread the other members. Arrows choose, Enter or Tab puts the name in, Escape closes. In a group chat, naming agents means only they answer, and the line under the composer says so ("Only Bo will answer"); a named agent always answers, and a message that names no agent reaches every agent as before. A member named sees "@" on that conversation in the rail until they open it. Names in messages stand out, your own most of all. The cloud reads the names from the text itself (migration `20260928010000_runtime_room_mentions`), so older browsers and the agents themselves need nothing new. Until the cloud has it, "@" lists nobody.
- See who is here. The faces of the other members who have a conversation open right now stand in its header, before the computer button: up to three, then "+2" for the rest, with their names on hover ("Sofie Lund and Mads Holm are here"). A face grows in when someone opens the conversation and goes when they leave, switch conversations or hide the tab. It runs on the company's private presence channel: each tab registers a device key and signs what it announces, and names come from the company's member list, so nobody can appear as someone else. Nothing is stored. A company with one member joins nothing.
- See what teammates are writing and write it with them. In an agent thread or a group chat, another member's unsent message shows as a notch with their picture right above the composer; its dots move while they type. Point at it and the notch opens up in place to show the message as it is written; click it (tap on a phone) to open it above the composer and write in it. Both see each other's words as they are typed, and words typed at the same moment are merged, never lost. Only the member who started the message can send it, as their own message on their own account; the open draft has no Send and says so. The owner's composer names who is writing in it with them, and words a teammate adds while the message is being sent stay for the next one. A reloaded browser gets its own unsent message back. The cloud holds each draft under one revision and pushes changes on a private channel only the room's members receive (migration `20260928000500_room_shared_drafts`). Nothing is shared in a company with one member, or until the cloud has the migration.
- Call a skill from the composer: type "/" and a menu above the message field lists the skills of this conversation, the agent's own first (labelled with its name), then the company's, each with its description. Keep typing to narrow it, move with the arrow keys, pick with Enter, Tab or a click, close with Escape. The picked "/skill-name" shows in blue, as does any name of a known skill typed by hand or restored with a draft, and in written messages: blue in an agent's or another member's bubble, a light chip in the member's own bubble (whatever its colour), never inside code or links; the agent loads that skill before acting on the message (runtime skills, contract 15.2). Skills are read with the member's own Company File access from `skills/` and `agents/<agent>/skills/`, parsed by the same SKILL.md reader the agents use, when "/" is first typed and again after a minute. A group chat offers the company's skills. The field stays a plain text box: while it names a skill, a copy of the text with the names in blue lies over its transparent characters.
- Agent threads run Fast by default, and the composer's ··· → Model menu has a Speed section with a Fast switch that turns it off for that one thread (migration `20260927161500_thread_fast_mode`; the runtime release that declares fast mode). Fast is the provider's priority tier: about 1.5x speed on GPT-6 Sol and Luna, 2x on GPT-6 Astra, which uses more of the plan; the switch's caption says which. The menu row reads, for example, "GPT-6 Sol · Low · Fast". Routines and other threads keep their own speed. Until the cloud runs threads on the Fast tier, the Speed section stays hidden. The Model panel may now grow taller so the models, effort and speed fit without scrolling.
- Rules and approval cards for agents' actions (runtime bundle v130, Grok Bot's Auto-review; the rules now sit at the bottom of Settings → General). A rule is one sentence: "When an agent wants to …, it should: Allow automatically | Ask first". Every member writes rules for themselves; owners and admins also write the company's rules, which everyone else sees locked. Before an agent acts outside the company (sends, pays, deletes, hires, sets up a routine, clicks or types on its computer), it is checked against them; Ask first wins when rules conflict, and built-in safety checks always apply. When an action needs approval, a card in the thread shows what the agent wants to do, why it waits and the exact action: the member it waits for (a personal rule's owner, or any owner or admin for a company rule or a built-in check) answers Allow once or Deny, and on a built-in check's card Always allow, which saves the proposed rule (editable first) for the company. Everyone else sees who it waits for. Until the cloud has v130, the rules section says so and no card offers controls.
- Fit a new profile picture to the circle before it is saved: after picking a photo, a dialog shows it behind the circle others will see. Drag it (or use the arrow keys) to move it, and zoom with the slider, the mouse wheel, a pinch or + and −; the photo always fills the circle. Save uploads that square, Cancel changes nothing. Before, the picture was always the photo's centre square.
- Hidden agents fold at the foot of the rail as "Hidden agents" with their count, as Grok Bot's "Hidden Bots" do: open it to list them, then Show in sidebar, Pin, Move to a section, or drag one out to show it again where it lands. The open or folded choice is remembered in this browser; the collapsed rail leaves the group out.

Settings → General shows it at the head of the Account card: click it to pick a photo, and once there is one the same click opens a small menu to pick another or go back to initials. The page shows pictures through `blob:` URLs, so its image policy is unchanged. Where the cloud has no member pictures yet, the disc stays initials with nothing to click.
- Choose the model and the thinking effort for one agent thread in the composer's ··· → Model menu: GPT-6 Astra, GPT-6 Sol or GPT-6 Luna (GPT-5.6 Sol and Terra are gone; a thread that used them goes back to the agent's default model) and Low, Medium or High effort (runtime bundle v91). ### Changed

- The agent's computer panel follows the machine its computer is on (a company can run several machines, each hosting up to six computers), names the machine by number once there is more than one, says why a starting machine is not up yet (being set up, or waiting for room on the servers) and moves when the agent gets room elsewhere. Panel copy uses the new words: an agent's computer (screen) runs on a company machine. Without the backend lookup it uses the company's one machine as before.
- An agent's approval card leads with what it wants to do and the change itself: who it reaches, what it is called and what it says. An email to send is shown as the thread's own email card, read-only (To, Cc, Subject and the whole text, read from the exact call), with Send email and Don't send. A Company File deletion reads "Delete “Old offer”" instead of a path. Why it asks is one quiet line; the call itself and the time of the request are not shown, and the answer buttons close the card. Once answered, the card folds to one line such as "Allowed once by Jonas. Send an email · anna@nordlys.dk".
- A coding session is one short line in the thread, like a message to a coworker: "Started a coding session in Codex" (or Claude Code), with the maker's own mark before the name. Click it to open the session as its own thread, where its steps, questions, answers and pull request live; the big block that unfolded in the thread, its status line and Show/Hide are gone, and so is the long "…'s coding session with you in … opened a draft PR" sentence. While a session is still going, the header shows it beside the agent: the agent's figure wearing the Codex or Claude mark, a little smaller than the agent's own and standing on the same line, with a thin divider between the figures; it works while the session works and waits while it asks for an answer. Hover or focus shows the session's title in a pill over the header (cut short with …); a click opens the session. The thread reads its sessions again every 2.5 s while one works (6 s while it only waits, 20 s in a hidden tab) and stops once they are all done.
- Codex is drawn with OpenAI's mark and Claude Code with the Claude spark, in the thread line, on the header figures and in the session's own thread; before, they had a hand-drawn prompt and a four-point star. The Claude card in Settings → Connections shows the Claude spark instead of Anthropic's "A".
- A coding session's own thread reads like a conversation. Codex or Claude Code speaks from the left behind its mark: its steps as quiet lines, what it says as bubbles (Markdown, as an agent's messages), its question as a bubble outlined while it waits, and the pull request as a card with Review changes, Open on GitHub and Ask … to review. Your follow-ups and answers stand on the right in your colour, the agent's in its figure's name. Day and time breaks come from the steps' own times. The composer is the conversation's, pinned under the transcript, which keeps the latest step in view unless you scroll up; the status line stands just above it and a question's choices and "Let … decide" sit between the two. Enter sends, Shift+Enter starts a new line, the caret lands in the composer when the session opens and takes input (on a touch screen it stays off, so no keyboard covers a session opened to be read), and a draft is kept when you go back and open the session again. The session's title, repository, branch and model open the transcript. A closing summary that repeats what the harness just said is shown once, and the "Draft PR opened" line gives way to the card. Older steps fold into one line only when at least three would hide, and new steps no longer push the oldest shown one under the fold; a long session is read to its end when it opens. A question's choices belong to the session's current turn only, so an older question's options are never offered for a new one. The footer ("Your answers and follow-ups go to Codex. Uses your Codex plan." with Close) is gone, as are the hint lines under the field; the back arrow and Escape return to the conversation. Other members get one lock line where the composer stands: "Only … can write to this session. Steer it through …".

### Fixed

- The rules list in Settings → General reads company rules that come from a company's `rules/` files (`createdVia: "folder"`, migration `20260929190000_folder_state_rules`). Before, one such rule made the whole list fail with "Rule is invalid.".
- A coding session past its 64th turn no longer fails to load: the web client no longer assumes a turn limit the cloud does not have.
- Settings → Friends only offers a friend request to an address that belongs to a Qorpera account. Typing a whole email looks it up once typing pauses: an account shows as a person under "Add a friend" and Send request lights up; any other address says "No Qorpera account uses …" and nothing can be sent, and your own address says so. Before, any well-formed address enabled Send request and the cloud refused it only after it was sent. An address counts once its owner has confirmed it (signed in with it), the whole address and never part of one, and a friend request follows the same rule (migration `20260928031000_friend_account_lookup`). Until the cloud has the migration, the search works as before.
- Closing an agent's computer before it has started no longer leaves it running for nothing. Opening the computer wakes it; closing the panel before the screen came up now takes that wake back (migration `20260927110000_runtime_office_wake_withdrawal`). The computer still finishes starting, since stopping a machine halfway through its boot is not safe, but once every member who woke it has closed it again and no agent or viewer needs it, it turns off one minute after it is up instead of after the usual five idle minutes. A wake whose screen connected, a first start and leaving the company send nothing. Until the cloud has the migration, closing works as before.
- Keep keyboard focus on Refresh in Settings → Connections while it reads usage. The button is greyed out and does nothing while a read runs, but stays focusable, so focus no longer drops to "Add account".
- A message still waiting for the agent now stands before the first message sent after it. Before, every waiting message sat below the whole conversation, so one the cloud never took (a "Queued" message from days ago) stayed at the bottom under every newer message and reply, with its old day break in the wrong place. A message sent while the agent is still writing stays at the bottom, below the agent's output, as before.
- A message the member stopped no longer shows the agent as failed. Before, Stop left a red disc and a "did not finish this run" line with Try again, and the composer lost its Stop for the agent's other work (such as a routine run in the same thread) until the line was dismissed. A stopped run now just ends: the agent shows what it is still doing, or idle, and Try again is not offered (to repeat the message, send it again). If Stop cut off Company File changes midway, the agent shows "Needs you" with Review, below any work still running or queued. The cloud side is migration `20260926192436_member_stop_settled_activity`; until it is live, the web marks such a stop as seen for the company once it has read the stop's details (members who can write only), so the work it hid, and that work's Stop, show again.
- A new stylesheet, script or picture that a Cloudflare edge did not have yet during a deploy is now answered with a "not found" that no cache keeps. Before, the edge kept the app page under that file's name, for up to a year under `/build/` (the app loaded without its styles on that edge until the file was renamed) and a day under `/assets/`. The build writes a small `404.html` into both folders, and fails if `_headers` lets caches keep files anywhere it cannot guard the same way. Every other path still opens the app.
- The app loads styled again where Cloudflare's edge had cached the page's HTML under the stylesheet's address (`build/app-QUCAFXYX.css`, requested while the #296 deploy was still spreading); the stylesheet now ships under a new name.
- Scrolling the rail or a thread no longer lays a tall fade over the Co-Founder card or the top message: the fade that marks more above is now 8 design px (about 10 on screen, was 36), close to the sidebar in the Claude app.
- A profile picture being saved is never deleted by a cleanup of leftover uploads that answered late; saving a picture after a very slow connection no longer fails and needs another try.
- Gmail and Google Drive in Settings → Connections → Services show their own logos instead of the Google "G".
- A right-click in the rail during the page's first 800 ms opens the agent or section menu; the long-press guard swallowed it before any press had opened one, which also made the rail menu tests fail at random in CI.
- Changing the figure of an agent that wears its figure's own colour now gives it the new figure's own colour, until a colour is picked in the same picker; a picked colour is kept through every figure. The first-run page keeps a colour chosen in the agent's details unless a different figure is picked there.
